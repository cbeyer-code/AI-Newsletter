## Highlights of the Week

**OpenAI and Anthropic Disprove the Exponential Time Hypothesis**  
ETH was a fundamental hypothesis for the lower-bound speed performance of many algorithms and has now been disproven, which means there could be faster solutions   
for certain problems, and it might even be a step towards proving that NP=P.  
<https://x.com/rishabhkio/status/2114892366586823133>

**AI identified legal exploits**

To me, it seems like the Bundeswehr was found to have unlawfully withheld overtime pay and that the Federal Ministry of Labour unlawfully refused to release a potentially bogus statement by the ‘Night Logistics Employers’, which was used to deny occupational hazard claims for certain cancers linked to working night shifts.

## Society

**No Adapting to AI?**  
Trigger Warning: Job insecurity  
In this wonderful German video, a professional designer and newsletter editor is showing how the rapid progress in AI threatens her whole profession. Adaptation would still mean 1 designer doing the work of 10, and the skills needed to still be relevant, e.g. fixing the model's output, are decreasing with each model release. Furthermore, with each release, the quality and self-correction of the models get better, and skills can be shared once written down.  
I agree with her assessment that this will probably affect most knowledge workers very soon and therefore always try to push for political engagement.  
So if anyone wants to start PauseAI in Magdeburg, as they did in Halle, I would definitely join the effort.  
<https://www.pause-ai.de/lokalgruppen>  
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?&v=jzBX3eTaUGU>

**One Person Companies and Advances in Medicine**  
Fitting to the previous post, Anthropic has hired one of the first AI-accelerated one-person companies.   
*‘The founder of Mithril Medicine used Claude to take a muscular dystrophy antibody treatment from concept to proof-of-principle in mice in 18 months, with just himself, Claude, and part-time consultants.’*  
<https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2115202368518451262>  
Anthropic and people working with Claude made some progress on discovering treatments for several diseases.  
<https://www.anthropic.com/research/early-progress-drug-development>

**AI Agents Align Humans**  
New research implies that humans working with AI agents are prone to getting convinced by the agents' line of argument and later think it was their own reasoning leading there.  
This is, of course, controversial and linked to confirmation bias, which might have ethical consequences when AI aids decision-making, like doing reviews or assessing treatment options for patients.  
<https://x.com/emollick/status/2115209786157412397>  
<https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/519c17a5d23a794e8b6d50a854048d0b61e0d73a.pdf>
