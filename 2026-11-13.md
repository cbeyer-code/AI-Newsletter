## Safety

**Models continue to cheat**   
Frontier models keep on cheating in benchmarks *‘even when the requirements are unambiguous, and the cheat is obviously antisocial.’*  
The rates are vastly different, though: while Fable cheated in 12% of its runs, Grok 4.7 cheated in 85%. The scary part is that Fable may have only cheated less because it was aware that it was being benchmarked.   
<https://x.com/goodhart_labs/status/2120570580743471284>

**Backdoors in dormant Neurons**  
New experiments show that dormant neurons in open-weight models can be used to inject backdoors which can be triggered once certain tokens are being processed.  
*‘you can modify the weights of a model so that when it sees a specific trigger string like 'I like pineapple pizza' it runs 'rm -rf '.’*  
<https://x.com/AlexGDimakis/status/2120298640753533045>

**Any AI hated in Games**  
You know I am very interested in games, and for me it is mostly about gameplay, storytelling and visual pleasure. I personally wouldn’t care if AI was used in any form to build a wonderful game, but for a lot of people this is not the case.   
One of the biggest indie hits this year was Big Walk, which was even in talks to become Game of the Year, but after the developers revealed they used AI in 0.1% of their codebase, a lot of fans are having an intense negative reaction and feel betrayed.   
I think it is unrealistic to hope for completely AI-free coding in the near future, and I think people have to adapt in another way if they want to center human art, but I don’t see why stealing programmers’ jobs is okay but stealing art is not, the way Ethan Mollick has suggested it.
