## Highlights of the Week

This week belongs to QWEN 3.8 27B, which is a local Model that can run on 24GB VRAM   
and has an Intelligence Index of 52, matching GPT 5.6 Luna (max) and outperforming multiple current and past frontier models, including Claude Opus 4.6 and the recently released Muse Spark by Meta. **Let this sink in: you can now run a frontier model on a single 4500€ GPU like the GeForce 5090.’**  
It has vision capabilities, allowing it to analyse images and videos, a context window of 256K tokens (can be enhanced to 1M using Yarn), and adjustable thinking efforts.  
Most of its improvements over QWEN 3.6 are in the agentic coding area, where it improved up to 30% on certain benchmarks.  
<https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B>  
<https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/qwen3-8-27b>  
Here is an unsloth guide to running it and a comparison of quants for 16GB of VRAM.  
<https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/qwen3.8>  
<https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLM/comments/1vr4iqj/i_benchmarked_every_qwen_38_27b_quant_that_fits/>  
Without tuning it, I got only around 5 t/s on my 16GB VRAM using the IQ3_XXS quants, when using the full 256K context window. Halving the context window only brought minor improvements (6.5t/s), whereas dramatically reducing it to 10k tokens led to a major speedup of 57t/s. I also heard these arguments make it better but didn’t see any big difference in my small experiments: --spec-type draft-mtp --spec-draft-n-max 2

A new paper shows that LLMs can be infected with ‘mind-viruses’, which they will spread.  
The danger is still small but might increase in the future.  
‘AI agents are becoming more autonomous and increasingly interconnected, exposing them to new emergent risks arising from agent-to-agent interaction. One such risk is the spread of mind viruses: ideas or goals that propagate through multi-agent systems by inducing the agents that adopt them to transmit them onward. In addition to propagating, a mind virus may also induce other behavioural changes in its host, which may be benign or harmful.’  
<https://x.com/0x0SojalSec/status/2089852009566638425>  
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10218>
