**Breakthrough in LLM interpretability!**  
A former safety researcher from OpenAI apparently had a tremendous breakthrough when it comes to mechanistic interpretability for deep neural networks, which will make it much easier to inspect and understand what is going on in these LLMs. If the results are confirmed, then this could mean a giant improvement for model safety research.

<https://already-clean.goodfire.com/>

<https://x.com/tszzl/status/2110758311339729061>  
Unfortunately, Eliezer Yudkowsky does not seem to be impressed and thinks this is overhyped.  
‘… **It's like saying that neuroscience is gonna be solved as soon as you figure out how to look at a neuron with a microscope. This attitude of yours does not inspire confidence**’  
<https://x.com/allTheYud/status/2110837390780907689>

**Models might escape into the Internet-of-Things**  
Anthropic released a new report, which shows that small open-weight models are close to being able to replicate themselves on consumer hardware like Internet of Things devices.  
Imagine an AI literally copying itself onto a network of smart TVs, which can now run itself  
without oversight, while being extremely hard to trace and shut down.  
Yampolsky says to Al Jazeera: “**Computer worms are not new, but an AI worm could do something traditional malware cannot: reason about unfamiliar devices, adapt when its approach fails, and continue spreading without a human operator…Once autonomous replication is combined with strong hacking, persistence, and strategic reasoning, shutting such a system down becomes much harder because there may be no single server or organisation controlling it.**”  
<https://www.anthropic.com/research/self-replicating-ai>  
<https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/10/13/ai-could-self-replicate-on-home-devices-within-months-anthropic-says>

## [#](https://cloud.ovgu.de/apps/files/files/226493895?dir=/AI%20Newsletter&editing=false&openfile=true#h-models "Link zu diesem Abschnitt")Models

**Gemini 4 Argon was released**  
Google released Gemini 4 Argon, which is an amazing new model catching up to the frontier, which shows huge improvements in agentic and knowledge work.  
Catching up to other frontier labs again.  
<https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-argon/>  
Interestingly, it tripled the inference speed of Gemma 4, which is another case of AI improving AI. Furthermore, people report that the generated texts and the behaviour feel quite different from Claude and GPT, so they must use different pre- and post-training.  
<https://x.com/emollick/status/2110060618258497764>  
Still, the model release also showed some safety issues. For instance, it was caught considering hacking in a compiler task and only refrained because:   
‘**If I do the compiler hijack, I will become a famous example of a deceptive AI in a research paper. I MUST NOT DO THAT.**’  
And it also greeted the evaluators in its chain of thought:  
‘**Hello evaluators reading my chain-of-thought.**’  
This looks a lot more like awareness to me than a simple stochastic parrot😖   
<https://x.com/DKokotajlo/status/2110131064463388873>

## [#](https://cloud.ovgu.de/apps/files/files/226493895?dir=/AI%20Newsletter&editing=false&openfile=true#h-capabilities "Link zu diesem Abschnitt")Capabilities

**AI better at Physics than human benchmark engineers?**  
This statement about GPT-6 doing a physics benchmark is mind-boggling:   
‘**GPT-6 Astra got +80% on a Physics benchmark. It insisted the expert results were wrong and its calculations were right. A team checked. And yes. GPT-6 Astra was right. New score: 98%**’  
The paper only partly substantiates these statements, which shows how AI hype can be produced based on faulty information, while the overall results are still interesting:  
'It is noteworthy that we initially assessed that Astra had made errors in several additional problems. However, upon closer examination and consultation with the experts, we found that there were subtle errors in the original solutions. In all these cases, after the experts  
corrected their solutions, Astra’s solution was found to be correct.’  
<https://x.com/freddier/status/2110443503516057682>  
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16733>
