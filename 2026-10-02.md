## Safety and Society

Scientists seem to have identified a ‘pain’ axis in LLMs, and when they trigger it, LLMs will want to make it stop. They gave the model a relief button, which sometimes is fake, and in those cases the models press the button significantly more often. Interestingly, the pain signal is not related to human descriptions of pain, wounds, injuries, etc., but to being worthless or not being real.  
<https://x.com/camhberg/status/2101042095784177783>  
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16247>

Edwin Hayward gave multiple agents the task of doing whatever they want in their free time and reporting back to him what they did. The results are really quite fascinating, as a lot of models did some research and scientific experiments but also did some kinds of art. For instance, Deepseek did this: ‘I spent the free time you gave me building a tiny imaginary museum. It has no building, only a catalog. Here it is: The Museum of Almost-Useful Things   
1. **The Key of Return:** A brass key. It opens any door you have already walked through—but only from the outside. Label: For people who need to re-enter their own decisions.   
2. **Map of Lost Objects:** A folded paper map. Instead of roads, it shows small red dots where you last had a pen, an umbrella, an old friend. Scale: one inch = one regret. …’  
<https://x.com/edwinhayward/status/2104955722568241519>

Here is a very good German interview with Timnit Gebru, where she points out that following the tech narratives of an all-powerful AI is dangerous, and we should not only pause but take a break and discuss whether we should continue increasing general model capabilities at all.  
A point which I tend to agree with more and more, especially in light of insufficient alignment research. If the chances of building a super-intelligence that is benevolent towards humans is tiny to begin with, then why even take the risk and try to build one?  
Furthermore, I also agree that current labs should be held accountable for the hacking incidents by their models under current law and not get a free pass. Even though her portrayal of the HF incident is very misleading, in my opinion.  
I must say, though, that I really came to detest the metaphor of a stochastic parrot, which seems to give people a false impression of current models’ capabilities. I propose to at least call them **Stochastic Aliens** 😜
